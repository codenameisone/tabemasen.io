Celiac (coeliac) disease in Japan

Japan's packaged-food labelling is among the strictest anywhere — but it covers wheat, not gluten. Barley and rye are on neither the mandatory nor the recommended allergen list, and outside one narrow rice-flour scheme, “gluten-free” has no enforced legal definition. For a celiac that changes what a label tells you, and what you have to check yourself.
